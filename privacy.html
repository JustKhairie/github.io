Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Kairos is a private, single-user tool. This policy explains how it handles Google user data.

What it accesses

With the account owner's permission, Kairos uses Google APIs to access the owner's own Google Calendar, Google Sheets, Google Drive, Gmail, Google Docs and Contacts.

How the data is used

Only to provide the assistant's features to its owner: reading and writing the owner's calendar events, reading and updating the owner's spreadsheets, and reading the owner's email and documents when the owner asks.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not transmitted to, sold to, or shared with any third party. No data is used for advertising.

Limited use

Kairos' use of Google user data is limited to the purposes above, in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time from their Google Account permissions page. Locally stored data can be deleted by the owner at any time.
